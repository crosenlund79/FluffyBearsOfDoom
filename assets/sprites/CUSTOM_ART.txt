Custom mode art
===============
Put your own sheets in the "custom" folder next to each template, using the
name without "template_". Anything you don't supply falls back to the HORROR
art (Horror is Custom's template), so copy the Horror template's size and cell
layout. Your files are never modified or overwritten.

Your file (in custom/)            Template it replaces (same layout)
bears/custom/bear.png             bears/horror/template_bear.png       Elk Bear (Player)
bears/custom/bunny.png            bears/horror/template_bunny.png      Bunny (Oswald)
bears/custom/dino.png             bears/horror/template_dino.png       Zombie Bear (Harald)
bears/custom/princess.png         bears/horror/template_princess.png   Skeleton Bear (Theodor, and the Princess Hunt)
tiles/custom/tiles.png            tiles/horror/template_tiles.png
objects/custom/bomb.png           objects/template_bomb.png            (no Horror version: shared)
objects/custom/fluff.png          objects/template_fluff.png           (shared)
objects/custom/duck.png           objects/template_duck.png            (shared)
objects/custom/chair.png          objects/horror/template_chair.png
objects/custom/clown.png          objects/horror/template_clown.png    (the clown only appears in Horror rounds)
powerups/custom/powerups.png      powerups/horror/template_powerups.png
effects/custom/effects.png        effects/horror/template_effects.png

Layouts (cell counts, sizes) are listed in README.md under "Replacing the art".
